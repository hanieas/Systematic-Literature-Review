# Together They Stand: A Systematic Review of Empirical Studies on LLM-Based Multi-Agent Applications in Education

## Abstract
The rapid advancement of large language models (LLMs) has created opportunities to design AI agents capable of natural language understanding. Recently, researchers have investigated the role of teamwork between AI agents and proposed multi-agent systems (LLM-MAS), including multiple agent instances, each configured with a specific role. The concept of LLM-MAS has generated significant interest in implementing educational applications across various learning and teaching domains. This systematic review aims to synthesize empirical studies published between 2023 and April 2026, examining the implementation and effectiveness of LLM-MAS in educational settings. After systematically searching databases such as ERIC, WoS, and Scopus, 72 relevant papers were located and included in the study. Our findings reveal that LLM-MAS are utilized across various educational contexts in five primary applications, including intelligent assessment and feedback systems, simulation-based learning systems, learning support tools, teaching support tools, and content development and instructional design, with learning support tools being particularly prominent. Based on our comprehensive review, this paper discusses benefits, existing challenges and future directions of LLM-MAS in education. The main identified benefits include improving the accuracy of generated content, reducing cultural bias, supporting complex and multidisciplinary problems. The main challenges include cascading hallucinations, system complexity, and computational cost. Future research is required to evaluate the long-term educational impact of LLM-MAS in real classroom environments, while also addressing challenges related to hallucination control, computational efficiency, and generalizability.

Keywords: Large language models (LLMs), Multi-agent system (MAS), Educational technology, AI in education
